Pack smart

Essential gear for CT day hikes

An easy-to-follow packing list so you can enjoy the woods with confidence.

Must-have items

  • Comfortable hiking shoes with grip
  • Layered clothing and lightweight rain shell
  • 2 liters of water and trail snacks
  • Map, compass, or fully charged phone

Safety & comfort

  • First aid kit, sunscreen, and insect repellent
  • Hat, sunglasses, and biodegradable wipes
  • Headlamp or flashlight for early starts
  • Trash bag to leave no trace

Ready-to-go hiking bundle

Pack these items in a small daypack and keep the weight under 15 lbs for a comfortable beginner experience.

Category What to pack
Navigation Trail map, phone with offline map, compass
Hydration Water bottle or hydration bladder, electrolyte snack
Protection Sunblock, bug spray, rain layer, hat
Extras Small first aid kit, snacks, waste bag, binoculars
