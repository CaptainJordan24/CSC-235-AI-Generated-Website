Top beginner hikes in Connecticut

Gentle hikes with views, well-marked trails, and parking close by.

Hawk Rock, Sleeping Giant

1.4 miles round trip, paved sections, iconic overlook. Great for families and new hikers.

  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Parking: Mount Carmel lot
  • Tip: Bring water and a camera for the ridge views.

Mount Tom, Litchfield Hills

2.3 miles loop with bird song and historic rock walls — an easy introduction to CT highlands.

  • Difficulty: Easy-Moderate
  • Parking: Mount Tom State Park
  • Tip: Wear sturdy shoes for roots and stones.

Whitaker Woods, Avon

2.5 miles of wide, shady trails perfect for a gentle walk through oak and pine.

  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Parking: Farmington Valley Trailhead
  • Tip: Use this trail for a gradual return to hiking after winter.

Beginner hiking safety checklist

  1. Tell someone your route and expected return time.
  2. Bring enough water, a snack, and weather-appropriate layers.
  3. Stay on marked trails and respect trail signs.
  4. Leave no trace: pack out trash, stay on durable surfaces.
